Frequently asked questions

The questions our support team hears most.

I paid but the app still shows a paywall. What gives?

Tap Restore purchases in Settings or on the paywall. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If it still fails, email support with your receipt and we will check RevenueCat and your account.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside Lookstead. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Lookstead, Cancel. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions.

I want a refund. How do I get one?

Refund requests go through the App Store or Google Play. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. We cannot issue store refunds from our side.

The app misread my photo or gave a useless answer.

Try a clearer photo, better lighting, and a specific question. If the answer says Demo, the live service is not connected on that build. Email support with what you asked and we can improve from your example.

The app keeps crashing or will not open.

Update to the latest version, restart your phone, and free some storage. If it persists, email support with your device model and iOS or Android version.

How do I ask my first question?

From Home, tap Take a photo or Choose from photos. Preview the image, pick or type your question, then submit. Camera and library access are requested only when you start that flow.
