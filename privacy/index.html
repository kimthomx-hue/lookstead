Privacy Policy

Summary: How Lookstead handles photos, questions, accounts and subscriptions.

Last updated: 2 November 2026. Contact: privacy@lookstead.com

Overview

Lookstead helps you understand items in your home using photos and plain-language questions. We process photos and questions to provide answers, store optional saved items, and manage subscriptions through Apple and our subscription partner (RevenueCat).

What we collect

  • Photos and questions you submit for analysis.
  • Onboarding preferences and saved home items (on your device and, when signed in, in your account).
  • Subscription status from Apple and our billing partner (we do not receive your full payment card number).
  • If you use Sign in with Apple or email, authentication identifiers needed to operate your account (Apple may provide a private relay email).
  • Coarse technical data (app version, device type) to keep the service reliable.

Sign in with Apple and email

You can use Lookstead without a named account; the app may start with a private session on your device. Sign in with Apple or email links that session so purchases and cloud data can follow you. We never receive your Apple ID password.

Location and professionals search

Core photo Q&A does not require location. If you choose to find nearby professionals, you may share location once for that search or enter a postal or ZIP code instead.

Photos and AI

Photos are used to answer your question on secure servers. We do not use your household photos to train general-purpose AI models unless we obtain separate explicit consent.

Your choices

  • Delete local data in the app: Settings → Privacy & data.
  • After sign-in: Delete account and cloud data in Settings removes server copies tied to your account. Cancel App Store subscriptions separately in iPhone Settings → Subscriptions.
  • Email privacy@lookstead.com to exercise UK/EEA rights.

Children

Lookstead is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

The in-app policy under Lookstead → Privacy may include additional detail. For terms of use, see Terms of service and Help.
