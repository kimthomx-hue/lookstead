Privacy Policy

Summary: How Lookstead handles photos, questions and household data.

Last updated: 30 October 2026. Contact: privacy@lookstead.com

Overview

Lookstead helps you understand items in your home using photos and plain-language questions. We process photos and questions to provide answers, sync optional cloud data when you sign in, and manage subscriptions through Apple, Google and our subscription partner.

Your choices

You can delete local data in the app settings. For full policy text as shown in the mobile app, open Lookstead → Privacy, or email us for a copy.
